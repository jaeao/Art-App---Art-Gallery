Fernando Amorsolo

Nicknamed the "Grand Old Man of Philippine Art," he was the first-ever to be recognized as a National Artist of the Philippines. Fernando Amorsolo Y. Cueto was a portraitist and painter of rural Philippine landscapes.

Napoleon Abueva

1930 - 2018 • National Artist for Sculpture

Napoleon Abueva

Known as the "Father of Modern Philippine Sculpture," Napoleon Abueva revolutionized sculpture in the Philippines by introducing modern and experimental styles. He worked with various materials such as wood, metal, stone, and concrete, pushing the boundaries of traditional sculpture. His works often explore form, space, and movement, making him a key figure in the evolution of contemporary Filipino art.
